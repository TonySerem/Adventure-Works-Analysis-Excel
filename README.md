## Adventure Works Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Adventure Works sales data using Microsoft Excel to create visual representations of the data to assess the company's 
performance over four years. The analysis is primarily intrested on profit,Specifically, on the analysis of 
products, customer locations(countries), and the time trends to identify any patterns or trends.

## Key findings
-The total profit was $126.29
- The top-5 profitable products were Mountain-200 black 46, Mountain-200 black 42, Mountain-200 silver 46, Mountain-200 black 38, and Mountain-200 silver 42 out of the 606 available products
- The most profitable product colors were Black, Red, and Silver respectively
- Products priced above $150 made 95.4% of the profit while products priced below $150 made 4.6% of the profit
- United States and Australia contributed 62.7% of the profit
- 67.1% of the profit came from 2007 and 2008
- Weekdays make up 72% of the total profit
